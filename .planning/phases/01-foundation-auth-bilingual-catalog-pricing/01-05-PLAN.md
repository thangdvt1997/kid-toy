<objective>
Give staff the ability to actually build the catalog: bilingual categories and brands, products carrying every facet the storefront will filter on, variants carrying SKU/barcode/carton specs, safety certifications, and media — all behind content-role RBAC and all enforcing that Vietnamese AND English content exist from the moment a record is created.

Purpose: CATALOG-09 is not "a language switcher exists" — PITFALLS.md's "looks done but isn't" checklist is explicit that bilingual content is only done when product/category content has real translated rows with a defined fallback. Making both locales mandatory at write time is the cheapest possible enforcement point; retrofitting translations onto an existing catalog is the expensive one.
Output: `/api/admin/categories`, `/api/admin/brands`, `/api/admin/products` (+ variants, certifications), `/api/admin/products/:id/media`.
</objective>

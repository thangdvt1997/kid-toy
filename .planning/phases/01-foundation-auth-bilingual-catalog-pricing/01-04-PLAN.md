<objective>
Complete the identity story: a business can apply for a B2B account with its tax ID, business type and licence document (AUTH-03), staff can move that application onto a pricing tier so Plan 06 can prove viewer-aware pricing, and any user can recover a forgotten password through a single-use emailed link (AUTH-05).

Purpose: AUTH-03's uploaded licence is the first genuinely sensitive document this platform stores — PITFALLS.md names "business documents exposed via a guessable URL" as a security mistake to prevent from day one, not to patch later. AUTH-05's reset token is the classic replay target. Both get the correct implementation on the first pass.
Output: `POST /api/business-accounts/register`, `GET /api/business-accounts/:id/licence`, `PATCH /api/admin/business-accounts/:id/approval`, `GET /api/admin/business-accounts`, `POST /api/auth/forgot-password`, `POST /api/auth/reset-password`.

Scope boundary: this plan builds ONLY the minimal approval transition needed to make CATALOG-06 demonstrable. The full dealer-application review queue, proof-document workflow and tier-assignment UI are B2B-01/B2B-02/B2B-03 and belong to Phase 4. Do not build them here.
</objective>

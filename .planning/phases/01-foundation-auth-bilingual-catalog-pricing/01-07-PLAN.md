<objective>
Expose the catalog to the outside world: one set of public endpoints that serve anonymous shoppers, logged-in retail customers and approved dealers alike, returning locale-correct content, viewer-resolved pricing and a stock-status indicator — the endpoint the storefront in Plan 08 consumes.

Purpose: this is where Phase 1's success criterion 5 becomes observable — "the same SKU shows retail price to an anonymous viewer and tier-resolved wholesale price to an approved B2B viewer, and customers can filter by age range, category, brand and origin." ARCHITECTURE.md is explicit that both channels hit the SAME catalog endpoints and the API decides what price to return from the auth context; two channel-specific catalog endpoints would be the start of the duplicated-catalog anti-pattern.
Output: `GET /api/catalog/products`, `GET /api/catalog/products/:slug`, `GET /api/catalog/facets`.

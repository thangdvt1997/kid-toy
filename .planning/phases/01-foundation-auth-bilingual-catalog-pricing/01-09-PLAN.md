Make every authentication capability built in Plans 03 and 04 reachable from a browser: login, retail registration, B2B registration with licence upload, password reset, and a session that survives a refresh — with tokens held in httpOnly cookies that client-side JavaScript can never read.

This plan also completes the CATALOG-06 demonstration loop: after a dealer logs in through this form, Plan 08's catalog pages show wholesale prices with no further work.

Scope guard: functional-but-unstyled, same as Plan 08. No design system.
Output: `/[locale]/login`, `/register`, `/register-business`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`, `/account`, plus `/api/session` route handlers.

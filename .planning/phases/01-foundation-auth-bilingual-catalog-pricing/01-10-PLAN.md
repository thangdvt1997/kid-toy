<objective>
Give staff a minimal but complete browser path to build a catalog entry end to end — product with facets and both languages, variant with SKU/barcode/carton spec, safety certification, images, retail and dealer prices, stock level — and to approve a pending dealer onto a tier.

Purpose: Phase 1's success criterion 4 says "Admin can create a product with … all viewable in both Vietnamese and English." RESEARCH.md Open Question 1 resolved this as a minimal functional admin UI, not Swagger alone and not Phase 7's governed console. This plan is also the interactive proof that the RBAC built in Plan 03 holds when a real browser session drives it.

Scope guard: functional-but-unstyled. No dashboards, no audit log, no Excel import/export, no bulk operations, no data grid library, no design system — those are ADMIN-01..04 in Phase 7.
Output: `/[locale]/admin`, `/admin/products`, `/admin/products/new`, `/admin/products/[id]`, `/admin/business-accounts`.
</objective>
